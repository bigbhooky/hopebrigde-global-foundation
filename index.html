A PURPOSE-LED FOUNDATION

HUMANITY FIRST · ALWAYS

A more hopeful future begins when we act together.

Hopebrigde Global Foundation is a humanitarian nonprofit initiative committed to creating meaningful opportunities, restoring hope and improving the quality of life of vulnerable individuals and underserved communities around the world.

We believe meaningful progress is built through practical support, respectful partnerships and a long-term commitment to people. Our work is guided by the belief that every person deserves to be seen, heard and given the opportunity to thrive.

PROPOSED ELIGIBILITY FRAMEWORK

Who may be eligible?

  • Applicants must be at least 18 years old.
  • Applicants must be individuals genuinely experiencing financial need.
  • Previous beneficiaries may be excluded from subsequent batches, where the published programme rules state so.
  • Successful applicants will receive an official award notification.
  • Grant amounts and disbursement arrangements will be clearly documented in each official announcement.

These are proposed criteria, not an active grant offer. No application fee is required to enquire about future programmes. Always verify announcements through official foundation channels.
