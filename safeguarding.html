OUR RESPONSIBILITY

Safeguarding

Hopebrigde Global Foundation recognises the importance of protecting the dignity, safety and rights of children and adults who may be at risk.

This page is a policy placeholder. Before undertaking activities or accepting volunteers, the organisation should adopt and publish a safeguarding policy, appoint an appropriate safeguarding lead, establish safe recruitment and reporting procedures, and comply with applicable law.

For immediate danger, contact the appropriate local emergency or protection services.
